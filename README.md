## 📐 Frameworks & Integration Strategies

When building a self-hosted or hybrid sales territory and quota planning infrastructure:

1. **Geospatial Territory Boundaries**: Use **Interactive-Territory-Mapping** or **Final-Approach** for initial polygon boundary creation and interactive map rendering.
2. **Quota & Capacity Modeling**: Embed **revops_calc.py** into your RevOps pipeline to check rep ramp curves against assigned quota targets before locking in annual targets.
3. **Advanced Territory Optimization**: Utilize **OptaPlanner** or **Pfizer Territory Optimization** (Gurobi) for complex mathematical balancing (minimizing drive times while equalizing revenue potential).
4. **CRM Workflow Automation**: Route territories automatically inside **Vtiger CRM** based on postal/zip code lookup tables and account parameters.

- **Community Curated**: This repository is maintained for informational and research purposes only.
